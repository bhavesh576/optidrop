How to convert HEIC to JPG without uploading — free & private

iPhone users constantly face the problem of HEIC files being rejected by government portals, exam forms, and university websites. OptiDrop's HEIC to JPG Converter solves this instantly. Simply open the tool on any device, drag your HEIC photo, and click Convert. Using the heic2any library running entirely in your browser, the conversion happens locally with zero server uploads. Your original iPhone photo quality is preserved, and you get a universally accepted JPG file ready for any portal. This works on Android, Windows, Mac, and iPhone browsers — no app installation or software download required. Whether you need to upload a passport photo, exam picture, or KYC document, OptiDrop converts HEIC to JPG in seconds with complete privacy.

Extract PDF to JPG locally — convert every PDF page to an image

Need to extract images from a PDF document? OptiDrop's PDF to JPG Extractor renders each page of your PDF as a separate high-quality JPG image — entirely in your browser using PDF.js. Upload any PDF, choose your preferred quality and resolution scale (up to 2x for print-ready sharpness), and download individual pages or all pages as a ZIP file. This is perfect for extracting mark sheets, admit cards, certificates, and multi-page forms that need to be submitted as individual images. No watermark, no file size limits beyond your device memory, and absolutely no server upload. Students preparing for NEET, JEE, UPSC, or SSC exams use this tool to extract individual pages from consolidated PDF documents for separate portal uploads.

300 DPI photo maker online — passport & visa photo resizer

Government portals and embassy websites require passport photos at exactly 300 DPI with precise pixel dimensions. OptiDrop's 300 DPI Photo Resizer handles this with one-click presets for India Passport (35x45mm at 413x531 pixels), US Visa (600x600 pixels), NEET/JEE exam photos (600x600), UPSC/OCI (200x230), and PAN Card (150x200). Simply upload your photo, select a preset or enter custom dimensions, and the tool crops, resizes, and outputs a print-ready image at 300 DPI. The embedded DPI metadata ensures printing shops and photo studios produce exactly the right physical size. No Photoshop, no complex software — just upload, preset, and download. Works on mobile phones and tablets too.

Add name and date on photo for exam forms — free stamp tool

Many Indian competitive exams (NEET, JEE, UPSC, SSC, Banking) require candidates to write their name and the date of the photograph on the bottom of their passport photo. OptiDrop's Name & Date Stamper overlays this text directly onto your image using HTML5 Canvas. Type your name, pick today's date, choose the position (bottom-left, bottom-center, or bottom-right), select font size and color, and click Stamp. The text is permanently burned into the image — no watermark, no branding. Download the stamped photo instantly for your exam application. This eliminates the need to visit a photo studio or use complex editing software.
