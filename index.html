Convert PNG to JPG locally without losing quality

Resize photos to exact passport dimensions for exams & visa forms

Enter your required dimensions, set the target KB (e.g., 50KB), and OptiDrop will first resize the image to your exact dimensions and then compress it to fit the file-size limit — all in one step, entirely inside your browser. No more cropping and compressing in two separate tools.
