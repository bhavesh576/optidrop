About OptiDrop

OptiDrop is a free online toolkit with 300+ browser-based tools for images, PDFs, documents, resumes, invoices, calculators, developers, health, fitness, education, and more. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — no file uploads, no sign-ups, no software installations, and no hidden fees. Your files and data never leave your device, making OptiDrop the most private way to process files online.

Whether you need to compress images for WhatsApp, convert HEIC photos from your iPhone to JPG, merge PDFs for email, build an ATS-friendly resume, generate professional invoices, calculate EMI or SIP returns, or resize passport photos to exact dimensions — OptiDrop has a dedicated tool for the job. All tools are optimized for mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers, and they work in every modern browser including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

OptiDrop was built to solve a common problem: most online tools require you to upload your files to a remote server, which raises serious privacy concerns — especially for sensitive documents like payslips, tax returns, medical records, and legal contracts. OptiDrop eliminates this risk by processing everything locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas, Web Workers, PDF.js, and the Web Crypto API. This client-side architecture also means tools work faster since there is no upload/download delay.

The platform is used by students, professionals, freelancers, small business owners, photographers, designers, developers, and everyday users across 190+ countries. Popular tools include Image Compressor (compress photos to exact KB limits), HEIC to JPG Converter (convert iPhone photos without losing quality), JPG to PDF (create PDF documents from images), Resume Builder (create ATS-friendly resumes with professional templates), Invoice Generator (create GST-compliant invoices), EMI Calculator (calculate monthly loan payments), and QR Code Generator (create custom QR codes instantly).

OptiDrop is completely free to use with no premium tiers, no watermarks, no file size limits, and no daily usage caps. The platform is supported by non-intrusive advertisements and is committed to maintaining a clean, fast, and distraction-free user experience. New tools are added regularly based on user feedback and trending needs.
