Privacy-First Approach

Your privacy is not a feature — it is a fundamental design principle. Every tool on OptiDrop is built to process data locally in your browser. Here is what that means in practice:

  • No file uploads: Your images, PDFs, and documents are processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • No sign-ups: You do not need to create an account, provide an email, or share any personal information to use any tool.
  • localStorage only: Form data is saved in your browser's localStorage for convenience. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time.
  • Optional analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand page views and improve the site. No personal data is collected through analytics.
