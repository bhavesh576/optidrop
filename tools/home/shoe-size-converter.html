How Do Shoe Size Systems Differ?

Different countries use different shoe sizing systems. US sizing uses separate scales for men and women. UK sizing is similar to US but typically 0.5 to 1 size smaller. EU sizing uses a continuous scale based on the Paris point (2/3 cm). CM (or Mondopoint) directly measures foot length in centimeters and is the most universal system.

Tips for Buying Shoes Online

When buying shoes internationally, always use the CM measurement as your reference since it is based on actual foot length. Measure your feet in the afternoon when they are slightly larger. Remember that shoe fit also depends on width, brand, and style, so size charts are a guide, not a guarantee.
