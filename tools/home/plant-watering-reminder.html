Free Plant Watering Reminder — Never Forget to Water Your Plants

OptiDrop's Plant Watering Reminder helps you keep track of when each of your plants needs water. Add your plants with their watering frequency, and the tool calculates the next watering date. When a plant is overdue, it gets a red badge so you know exactly which plants need attention first.

How Does It Work?

Add a plant by entering its name and how often it needs to be watered (in days). The tool records today's date as the last watered date. Each time you water a plant, click the Water button to update the date. The tool calculates when the plant needs water next and highlights overdue plants in red.

Data Stored Locally

All your plant data is saved in your browser's localStorage. Your plants, watering schedules, and history persist between page visits. No account is needed and no data is sent to any server. This is 100% private plant care tracking.
