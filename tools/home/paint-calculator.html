Free Paint Calculator Online — Estimate Paint Gallons Needed

OptiDrop's Paint Calculator helps you estimate exactly how many gallons of paint you need for your room. Enter the room dimensions, number of doors and windows, and the number of coats. The calculator uses a standard coverage rate of 350 square feet per gallon and gives you an accurate estimate so you can buy the right amount of paint.

How Does the Paint Calculator Work?

The calculator first computes the total wall area by multiplying the room perimeter (2 times length plus 2 times width) by the room height. It then subtracts the area of doors (21 sq ft each) and windows (12 sq ft each) to get the paintable area. This area is multiplied by the number of coats, then divided by 350 sq ft per gallon to determine the gallons needed.

Tips for Accurate Paint Estimation

Always round up to the nearest gallon when purchasing paint. If your walls are textured or porous, consider adding 10-20% more paint. For dramatic color changes (dark to light or vice versa), use a tinted primer first and plan for 2-3 coats. Buy all your paint from the same batch to ensure consistent color throughout the room.
