Frequently Asked Questions

To convert kilometers to miles, multiply the distance in km by 0.621371. For example, 10 km x 0.621371 = 6.21371 miles. This converter does the calculation instantly — select Length, choose km as the input unit and miles as the output unit, then enter your value.
There are approximately 2.20462 pounds in 1 kilogram. To convert kg to pounds, multiply the weight in kg by 2.20462. To convert pounds to kg, multiply by 0.453592. This converter handles both directions automatically.
To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply the temperature by 1.8 (or 9/5) and then add 32. For example, 100 C x 1.8 + 32 = 212 F. To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius, subtract 32 and then multiply by 5/9.
Most unit conversions use a simple multiplication factor. You multiply the input value by a conversion factor to get the output. For example, 1 km = 1000 meters (factor: 1000), 1 mile = 1.60934 km (factor: 1.60934). Temperature is the exception, requiring an offset in addition to multiplication.
