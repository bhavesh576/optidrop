Free EMI Calculator Online — Home Loan, Car Loan & Personal Loan

OptiDrop's EMI Calculator is a free online tool that helps you calculate your Equated Monthly Installment for any type of loan. Whether you are planning to take a home loan, car loan, personal loan, or education loan, this calculator gives you an accurate estimate of your monthly payment, total interest payable, and a year-by-year breakdown of your repayment schedule.

How Does the EMI Calculator Work?

The calculator uses the standard EMI formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]. Simply enter your loan amount, annual interest rate, and tenure in months or years. The tool instantly computes your monthly EMI, total payment over the loan period, and the total interest you will pay. A detailed yearly breakdown table shows how your principal and interest portions change over time.

Supports All Loan Types

This EMI calculator works for home loans, car loans, personal loans, education loans, gold loans, and any other fixed-rate installment loan. You can toggle between months and years for the tenure input. All calculations happen instantly in your browser — no data is sent to any server, ensuring complete privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount you pay every month toward your loan until it is fully repaid. Each EMI includes both the principal amount and the interest charged by the lender.
EMI is calculated using the formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12), and N is the number of monthly installments.
