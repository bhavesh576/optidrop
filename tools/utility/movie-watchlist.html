Free Movie Watchlist Online — Organize Your Movie List

OptiDrop's Movie Watchlist is a free online tool that helps you organize movies you want to watch. Add movies with genre, priority level, and personal ratings. Filter by genre or priority and sort by title, priority, or rating to find the perfect movie for any mood.

How Does the Movie Watchlist Work?

Enter the movie title, select a genre, set a priority level (High, Medium, Low), and optionally rate it from 1 to 5 stars. Your watchlist is displayed as a clean card layout with all the details. Use the filter and sort options to quickly find movies by genre, priority, or rating. All data is saved to your browser's localStorage.
