Frequently Asked Questions

PIN stands for Postal Index Number. It is a 6-digit code used by India Post to identify specific geographic regions for efficient mail delivery. The first digit represents the postal zone, the first two digits represent the sub-zone, and the last three digits identify the specific sorting office or delivery post office.
India has over 19,000 PIN codes covering more than 150,000 post offices across all states and union territories. This tool includes a curated database of major city pincodes. For a complete directory, visit the official India Post website.
The first digit indicates one of 9 postal regions (1 for Delhi, 2 for UP/Uttarakhand, 3 for Rajasthan/Gujarat, 4 for Maharashtra/Chhattisgarh/MP, 5 for South India, 6 for East India, 7 for Northeast, 8 for Bihar/Jharkhand, 9 for Army Post Office). The first two digits together indicate the sub-region or state, and the last three digits identify the specific delivery post office.
This tool contains a curated dataset of major Indian pincodes covering key cities and areas. While we strive for accuracy, pincodes may change or new ones may be assigned. For official and complete pincode data, please refer to the India Post website at indiapost.gov.in.
