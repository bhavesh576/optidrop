About Pack Weight Calculator

Avoid overweight baggage fees by planning your luggage weight in advance. This tool lets you add each item you plan to pack, see the total weight, and compare it against your airline's baggage limit. The progress bar changes color as you approach the limit, and warnings appear when you exceed it.

Most major airlines allow 23 kg (50 lbs) for checked baggage in economy class. Budget airlines may have lower limits or charge extra for checked bags. Business and first class often allow 32 kg (70 lbs). Always verify with your specific carrier before traveling.

You can save your packing lists to your browser for future trips. This is perfect for frequent travelers who pack similar items. All data is stored locally on your device and never sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard airline baggage weight limit?

Most airlines allow 23 kg (50 lbs) for checked baggage on economy class. Some airlines allow up to 32 kg (70 lbs) for business or first class. Always check your specific airline's policy.

Can I save my packing list?

Yes, this tool saves your packing list to your browser's local storage. You can save multiple lists and load them anytime. Your data never leaves your device.

What weight units are supported?

You can enter item weights in grams, kilograms, or pounds. The tool automatically converts everything to show totals in both kg and lbs.

How do I avoid overweight baggage fees?

Use this calculator to weigh items before packing. Set your airline's weight limit and monitor the remaining allowance. The tool warns you when you're approaching or exceeding the limit.
