Free Recipe Scaler Online — Adjust Recipe Servings Instantly

OptiDrop's Recipe Scaler is a free online tool that helps you scale recipe ingredients up or down. Whether you are cooking for a large party or just need a smaller portion, simply enter the original servings, your desired servings, and your ingredient list. The tool automatically adjusts all quantities proportionally.

How Does the Recipe Scaler Work?

Enter the original number of servings your recipe makes, then add each ingredient with its quantity, unit, and name. Enter your desired number of servings and click Scale Recipe. The tool calculates a scaling factor (new servings divided by original servings) and multiplies each ingredient quantity by that factor. It preserves your original units and ingredient names.

Tips for Scaling Recipes

When scaling up baking recipes, be cautious with leavening agents like baking powder and baking soda -- they may not scale perfectly. Spices and seasonings should be scaled conservatively (use about 1.5x instead of 2x when doubling). Cooking times may need adjustment for larger or smaller batches. All calculations happen in your browser with no data uploaded anywhere.
