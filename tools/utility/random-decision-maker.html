About Random Decision Maker

When you're stuck between choices, let the wheel decide! This animated decision maker creates a colorful spinning wheel from your options. Simply enter your choices (one per line), build the wheel, and spin to get a random result.

Perfect for picking restaurants, choosing activities, deciding who goes first in a game, selecting raffle winners, or any situation where you need a fair random choice. The wheel uses weighted random selection so every option has an equal chance.

Enable the "Remove option after picked" feature for raffles or sequential selections. The history log tracks all your results so you can review past spins. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the decision wheel work?

Enter your options (one per line), then click Spin. The wheel rotates with a smooth animation and randomly selects one option. The result is displayed prominently and added to your history.

Can I remove an option after it's picked?

Yes, there is an option to automatically remove picked items from the wheel. This is useful for raffles or sequential selections where each option should only be picked once.

Is the selection truly random?

Yes, the wheel uses JavaScript's Math.random() for fair random selection. The animation is purely visual - the result is determined randomly before the wheel starts spinning.

Can I use this for giveaways or raffles?

Absolutely! Enter participant names, enable the remove-after-pick option, and spin for each winner. The history tracks all previous picks so you can verify the results.
