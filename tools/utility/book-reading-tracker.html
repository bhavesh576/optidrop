Free Book Reading Tracker Online — Track Your Reading Progress

OptiDrop's Book Reading Tracker is a free online tool that helps you track your reading progress across multiple books. Enter the book details and your current page, and the tool shows your reading percentage, pages remaining, and an estimated finish date based on your daily reading pace. All data is saved to your browser's localStorage.

Track Multiple Books

Reading more than one book at a time? No problem. Add as many books as you like to your reading list. Each book is tracked independently with its own progress bar, page count, and estimated completion date. Update your current page anytime to see your latest progress.

Stay Motivated

Seeing your reading progress visualized with a progress bar can be a powerful motivator. The estimated finish date helps you set realistic goals and stay on track. All data is stored locally in your browser, so your reading list is always available without needing to create an account. 100% private, no data uploaded anywhere.
