About Habit Tracker

Building good habits is one of the most powerful ways to improve your life. This simple habit tracker helps you stay accountable by showing your daily progress, weekly completion rate, and current streak for each habit.

The tracker displays the current week (Monday through Sunday) with checkboxes for each day. Check off habits as you complete them and watch your streaks grow. The stats bar shows your overall progress at a glance.

All your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This means your habits and progress are private and persist between sessions. No account needed, no data sent to any server.

Tips for Building Habits

Start small with 1-3 habits. Be specific (e.g., "Read 10 pages" instead of "Read more"). Track consistently and celebrate streaks. If you miss a day, don't break the chain twice - get back on track immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the habit tracker work?

Add your habits, then check off each day as you complete them. The tracker shows your current week, streak count for each habit, and overall completion percentage. All data is saved in your browser.

Is my data saved?

Yes, all habit data is saved to your browser's local storage. It persists between sessions but is tied to your specific browser. Clearing browser data will remove your habits.

How is the streak calculated?

A streak counts consecutive days a habit was completed. If you miss a day, the streak resets. The tracker shows your current streak for each habit.

Can I track multiple habits?

Yes, you can add as many habits as you like. Each habit gets its own row in the weekly grid with individual checkboxes for each day and its own streak counter.
