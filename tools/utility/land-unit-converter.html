Frequently Asked Questions

Bigha is a traditional land measurement unit used across India, but it was never standardized nationally. Different regions adopted different sizes based on local agricultural practices. For example, 1 Bigha in UP is about 27,000 sq ft, while in Punjab it's only about 9,070 sq ft. Always confirm which Bigha standard applies to your land.
