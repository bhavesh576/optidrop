About the Date Calculator

A date calculator is one of the most useful everyday tools. Whether you need to know how many days are left until a deadline, how long it has been since a specific event, or want a live countdown to a future date, this tool handles it all instantly in your browser.

Days Between Dates mode lets you pick any two dates and see the exact difference broken down into years, months, weeks, and days. It also shows the total number of days, hours, and minutes between the two dates. This is perfect for calculating project timelines, contract durations, pregnancy weeks, or simply satisfying curiosity about time spans.

Countdown mode displays a live, ticking countdown to any future date and time you choose. It updates every second and shows days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining. Use it to count down to birthdays, holidays, product launches, exams, or any important milestone.

Like all OptiDrop tools, this date calculator runs entirely in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server, and no sign-up is required. Your dates and calculations stay 100% private on your device.
