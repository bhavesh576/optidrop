Free Unit Price Comparator Online — Find the Best Deal

OptiDrop's Unit Price Comparator is a free online tool that helps you compare the price per unit across multiple products. Enter up to 5 products with their prices, quantities, and units, and the tool calculates the unit price for each and highlights the cheapest option. Perfect for smart grocery shopping and finding the best value.

How Does the Unit Price Comparator Work?

Enter each product's name, price, quantity, and unit of measurement. The tool converts all quantities to a common base unit (grams for weight, milliliters for volume), then divides the price by the converted quantity to get the price per base unit. The product with the lowest unit price is highlighted as the best deal.

Smart Shopping Tips

Always compare unit prices, not just shelf prices. A larger package is not always cheaper per unit. Stores sometimes price smaller packages lower per unit to move inventory. Use this tool every time you shop to make sure you are getting the best value. All calculations happen in your browser with no data uploaded anywhere.
