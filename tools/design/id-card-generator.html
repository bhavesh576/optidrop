OptiDrop's ID Card Generator lets you create professional identification cards directly in your browser. The ID card is rendered using HTML5 Canvas at 1016 x 638 pixels, which is equivalent to 300 DPI at standard credit card dimensions. The downloaded PNG is crisp and suitable for printing on card stock. All processing happens in your browser, so your data and photos remain completely private.
