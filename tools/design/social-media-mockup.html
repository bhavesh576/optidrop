Free Social Media Mockup Generator — Preview Your Posts

OptiDrop's Social Media Mockup Generator lets you preview how your text will appear on major social media platforms before you hit "Post." Choose from Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook templates to see a realistic preview of your content.

Character Count with Platform Limits

Each platform has different character limits. Twitter/X allows 280 characters, Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters, LinkedIn allows 3,000, and Facebook permits up to 63,206 characters. The tool shows a real-time character count and a color-coded progress bar that warns you as you approach the limit.

Customize Display Name and Handle

Enter your display name and username/handle to see how your profile will appear alongside your post. This helps you create a complete mockup for client presentations, content planning, or personal review.

100% Private — No Data Uploaded

Everything runs in your browser. No text, names, or handles are sent to any server. Use this tool freely for social media planning, content creation, and client mockups.
