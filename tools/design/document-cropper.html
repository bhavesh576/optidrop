Free Document Cropper Tool

Easily crop documents from photos and scanned images. Upload your image, draw a rectangle around the area you want to keep, and download the cropped result. Perfect for isolating documents from messy backgrounds.

How to Crop Documents

Upload your image by clicking the upload area or dragging and dropping a file. Once loaded, click and drag on the image to draw the crop rectangle. Adjust the corners by dragging them. Click Download to save the cropped result.

Supports Multiple Formats

Upload JPG, PNG, WebP, or BMP images. Download your cropped document as PNG (lossless) or JPG (compressed) format.

No Upload Required

All cropping happens in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images are never sent to any server, keeping your documents completely private.
