Free Online Employee ID Card Generator

OptiDrop's Employee ID Card Generator creates professional employee identification cards with company branding. Upload your company logo and employee photo, enter the employee details including name, ID number, designation, department, blood group, and emergency contact, and generate a print-ready ID card at standard credit card dimensions.

Front and Back Card Design

The generator produces both front and back sides of the employee ID card. The front displays the company logo, employee photo, name, and designation. The back shows the company address, emergency contact, blood group, and ID number. The cards are rendered at 1016 x 638 pixels (300 DPI at credit card size) and can be downloaded as PNG files for printing on card stock or PVC card printers.
