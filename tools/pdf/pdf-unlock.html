Unlock PDF Files Online — Free, No Upload Required

OptiDrop's PDF Unlocker is a free online tool that removes password protection from your PDF documents when you know the password. Whether you need to remove a user password that restricts opening the file, or an owner password that prevents printing, copying, or editing, our tool handles both types of PDF encryption. Unlike other online services, OptiDrop processes everything locally in your browser — your PDFs are never uploaded to any server, making it completely safe for confidential documents like contracts, financial records, and personal files.

How PDF Unlocking Works

Our tool uses the pdf-lib JavaScript library to load your password-protected PDF, decrypt it using the password you provide, and then save a new copy without any encryption or restrictions. The entire process happens in your browser's memory — nothing is sent over the internet. The unlocked PDF preserves all original content, formatting, images, and layout exactly as they were in the protected version.

User Password vs Owner Password

PDF files can have two types of passwords. A user password (also called "open password") is required to open and view the document. An owner password restricts actions like printing, copying text, or editing. Our tool can remove both types when you provide the correct password. Simply enter the password you know, and the tool will produce a fully unlocked copy with all restrictions removed.

Completely Private and Secure

Your privacy is our top priority. Since all processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript, your PDF file and its password never leave your device. There is no server upload, no cloud processing, and no data collection. This makes our tool ideal for unlocking sensitive documents such as legal contracts, financial statements, medical records, and any other confidential PDF files.
