Compress PDF — Reduce PDF File Size Online

Shrink your PDF files without losing too much quality. Our advanced compressor renders each page at optimized resolution and re-embeds them as compressed images. Choose from 4 compression levels to find the perfect balance between file size and quality.

How PDF Compression Works

The tool uses pdf.js to render each PDF page to a canvas element at a resolution determined by your chosen compression level. It then converts those canvases to JPEG images and uses pdf-lib to assemble a new, smaller PDF. This approach is especially effective for PDFs with large images or complex graphics.

Compression Levels Explained

Low: Minimal compression at high DPI. Best for documents where quality matters most.

Medium: Balanced compression. Good for most everyday documents.

High: Aggressive compression at lower DPI. Good for email attachments.

Maximum: Smallest possible file size. Quality may be noticeably reduced.
