Add Page Numbers to PDF — Free, No Upload Required

OptiDrop's PDF Page Numbering tool lets you insert page numbers into any PDF document in seconds. Whether you need to number pages for a thesis, report, legal document, or business proposal, our tool provides complete control over placement and formatting. Unlike online tools that require uploading your files, OptiDrop processes everything locally in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library — your PDFs are never uploaded to any server, making it completely safe for confidential documents.

How PDF Page Numbering Works

Our tool uses pdf-lib to load your PDF file, iterate through every page, and draw the page number text at your chosen position. The library embeds Helvetica font (built-in) so no external font files are needed. The numbers are drawn directly onto the PDF pages as vector text, which means they remain crisp and sharp at any zoom level — unlike rasterized solutions.

Customizable Options

Choose from six position options (top and bottom, left, center, right), three font sizes, and four number formats. Set a custom starting number for documents that continue from a previous section. The "Page 1 of N" format is especially popular for formal reports and academic papers where readers need to know the total page count.
