Split PDF Files Online — Free, Private, No Upload

OptiDrop's PDF Splitter is a free online tool that lets you split any PDF document into individual pages or extract specific page ranges. Whether you need to extract a single page from a 100-page report, split a multi-chapter document into separate files, or pull specific pages for sharing, our tool handles it all. Unlike other online splitters, OptiDrop processes everything locally in your browser using the powerful pdf-lib library — your PDFs are never uploaded to any server, making it completely safe for confidential documents like contracts, medical records, and financial statements.

Two Split Modes for Maximum Flexibility

Choose Page Range mode to extract specific pages using simple notation like "1-3, 5, 7-10". This creates a single new PDF containing only the pages you specified. Or use Individual Pages mode to split every page of your PDF into a separate file — perfect for extracting chapters, separating scanned documents, or preparing pages for individual distribution. All split files can be downloaded individually or as a convenient ZIP archive.

How PDF Splitting Works

When you upload a PDF, our tool reads the file structure using pdf-lib, a robust JavaScript library for PDF manipulation. It parses the page tree, extracts the pages you selected, and creates new PDF documents with the exact same formatting, fonts, and quality as the original. The entire process happens in your browser's memory — no server round-trips, no waiting for uploads, and no risk of data interception.

Perfect for Every Use Case

No matter your need, OptiDrop delivers fast, accurate results while keeping your files completely private.
