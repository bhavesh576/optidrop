Add Text Watermarks to PDF Online — Free, No Upload Required

OptiDrop's PDF Watermark tool lets you add professional text watermarks to your PDF documents in seconds. Whether you need to stamp "CONFIDENTIAL" on legal documents, add "DRAFT" to work-in-progress files, or brand your PDFs with a company name, our tool handles it all. Unlike other online watermark tools, OptiDrop processes everything locally in your browser — your PDFs are never uploaded to any server, making it completely safe for sensitive documents like contracts, financial reports, and intellectual property.

Full Customization Control

You have complete control over how your watermark looks. Adjust the font size from 12px to 120px for anything from subtle corner stamps to bold page-spanning overlays. Set opacity from 10% for barely-visible background marks to 100% for prominent text. Rotate the watermark at any angle from 0 to 360 degrees — the classic diagonal watermark at 45 degrees is just a slider away. Choose any color to match your branding or make the watermark stand out.

Watermark Every Page Automatically

The watermark is automatically applied to every page of your PDF, centered on the page regardless of page size or orientation. Whether your document has 1 page or 500 pages, each one gets the same consistent watermark. This is ideal for batch-processing documents that need uniform branding or confidentiality markings across all pages.

How PDF Watermarking Works

Our tool uses the pdf-lib JavaScript library to read your PDF, embed a standard font, and draw the watermark text on each page using the drawText() method with your chosen font size, color (with opacity), rotation angle, and centered positioning. The modified PDF is then generated in memory and offered for download. The entire process happens in your browser — no server uploads, no file storage, no privacy concerns.
