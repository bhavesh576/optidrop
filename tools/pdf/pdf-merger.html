Merge PDF Files Online — Free, No Upload Required

OptiDrop's PDF Merger is a free online tool that combines multiple PDF documents into a single file. Whether you need to merge invoices, combine contract pages, join reports, or consolidate academic papers, our tool handles it all — completely free and entirely in your browser. Unlike other online mergers, OptiDrop processes everything locally on your device. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server, making it completely safe for confidential documents like business contracts, financial statements, and personal records.

Drag, Drop, Reorder, and Merge

Simply drag and drop your PDF files into the upload zone, or click to browse your device. Once uploaded, you can reorder files by dragging them or using the move up and move down buttons. Each file displays its page count, so you know exactly what you are merging. When you are happy with the order, click Merge PDFs and your combined document will be ready to download in seconds. The tool uses the pdf-lib library to copy pages directly, preserving the original quality of every page.

Works on Any Device

The PDF Merger works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone directly in your web browser. No software installation, no sign-up, and no file size restrictions beyond your device memory. It is perfect for students combining assignment pages, professionals merging project reports, or anyone who needs to consolidate multiple PDFs into one organized document.
