Rotate PDF Pages Online — Free, No Upload

OptiDrop's PDF Page Rotator lets you fix page orientation in any PDF document. Whether you scanned a document sideways, received a PDF with rotated pages, or need to adjust orientation for printing, this tool handles it instantly. You can rotate all pages at once or target specific pages using page numbers or ranges.

How PDF Page Rotation Works

Upload your PDF, choose the rotation angle (90° clockwise, 90° counter-clockwise, or 180°), select whether to rotate all pages or specific ones, and click Rotate. The tool uses pdf-lib to apply rotation at the page level without re-encoding content, ensuring zero quality loss. The rotated PDF downloads automatically.

100% Private — Files Never Leave Your Device

All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. No files are uploaded to any server. Your documents remain completely private and secure.
