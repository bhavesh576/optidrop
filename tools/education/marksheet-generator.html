Online Marksheet Generator

Our marksheet generator helps students, teachers, and educational institutions create professional mark sheets quickly and easily. Simply enter the student's details, add subjects with marks obtained and total marks, and the tool automatically calculates the overall percentage and assigns a grade.

Automatic Grade Calculation

The tool uses a standard grading scale: A+ (90%+), A (80-89%), B+ (70-79%), B (60-69%), C (50-59%), D (40-49%), and F (below 40%). This grading system is widely recognized and follows common academic standards used by many education boards.

How to Use This Tool

Enter the student's name, institution, roll number, and exam name. Then add subjects one by one, entering the marks obtained and total marks for each. The summary section shows the running total, percentage, and grade in real-time. When ready, preview the marksheet and download it as a PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the marksheet generator work?
Enter the student's name, institution, exam name, and add subjects with their marks obtained and total marks. The tool automatically calculates the total marks, percentage, and assigns a grade based on the percentage. You can then preview and download the marksheet as a PDF.
How is the grade calculated?
Grades are assigned based on the percentage: 90% and above is A+, 80-89% is A, 70-79% is B+, 60-69% is B, 50-59% is C, 40-49% is D, and below 40% is F (Fail). This grading scale follows a common standard used by many educational boards.
Can I add multiple subjects?
Yes, you can add as many subjects as needed. Click the 'Add Subject' button to add new rows. Each row has fields for the subject name, marks obtained, and total marks. You can also remove any subject row by clicking the remove button.
Is the marksheet generator suitable for all education boards?
The generator creates a general-purpose marksheet format that works for most education boards including CBSE, ICSE, state boards, and universities. You can customize the institution name, exam name, and other fields to match your specific board's requirements.
