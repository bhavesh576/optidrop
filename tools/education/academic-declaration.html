Free Academic Declaration and Undertaking Generator

Academic declarations and undertaking letters are formal documents required by educational institutions for various purposes. This tool helps you generate properly formatted declaration letters quickly and professionally.

Common Types of Declarations

Declaration of Originality: Used for thesis and project submissions to confirm the work is original.
Anti-Ragging Undertaking: Required during admissions to pledge no involvement in ragging.
Fee Undertaking: For students receiving scholarships or fee waivers.
General Undertaking: For hostel accommodation, lab usage, or other institutional requirements.

How to Use

Enter your name, course, institution, and the date. Then write your declaration text in the provided field. The tool will format it as a formal letter with proper header, body, and signature section. Preview the result and download as PDF.

Legal Note

While this tool generates a properly formatted declaration, ensure that the content accurately reflects your intentions. A declaration is a legally binding statement, so only include truthful and accurate information.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an academic declaration?
An academic declaration is a formal letter where a student declares certain facts or undertakes specific responsibilities as required by an educational institution.
When do I need an undertaking letter?
Undertaking letters are commonly required during admissions, project submissions, exam registrations, hostel accommodation, and for compliance with institutional policies like anti-ragging.
Can I customize the declaration text?
Yes, the tool provides a free-text field where you can enter your own declaration text or use the provided placeholder as a starting point.
Is the format accepted by institutions?
The generated declaration follows the standard formal letter format accepted by most schools and colleges in India. You can further customize it to match your institution's specific requirements.
