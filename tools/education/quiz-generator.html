Free Online Quiz Generator and Test Maker

Our quiz generator lets you create custom multiple choice quizzes in seconds. Whether you are a student preparing for exams or a teacher creating practice tests, this tool makes quiz creation simple and fast.

How to Create a Quiz

Switch to Create mode, give your quiz a title, and start adding questions. Each question needs a question text and four answer options. Select the correct answer by clicking the radio button next to it. You can add as many questions as you need and remove any question with the delete button.

Taking a Quiz

Switch to Take mode and select any quiz you have previously saved. Answer all questions by clicking your chosen option, then submit to see your score. The results show which answers were correct and incorrect, helping you learn from your mistakes.

Save and Load Quizzes

All quizzes are saved in your browser's localStorage, so they persist between sessions without needing an account. You can save, load, edit, and delete quizzes at any time. Data stays entirely on your device for complete privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a quiz?
Switch to Create mode, enter your quiz title, then add questions one by one. Each question needs 4 answer options, and you select the correct one. Click Save Quiz when done.
Where are my quizzes saved?
Quizzes are saved in your browser's localStorage. They remain available until you clear your browser data. No account or sign-in required.
Can I edit a saved quiz?
Yes, you can load any saved quiz, make changes in Create mode, and save it again. The updated version will replace the previous one.
How does scoring work?
When you take a quiz, you answer all questions and submit. The tool shows your score as a percentage and highlights correct and incorrect answers for learning.
