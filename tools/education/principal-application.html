Free Formal Application Generator for Students

Whether you need to request a fee concession, transfer certificate, bonafide certificate, or any other matter, this tool helps you draft a properly formatted application to the principal of your school or college.

How It Works

Enter your name, class, institution, the subject of your application, and the body text explaining your request. The tool formats everything into a standard formal letter with proper salutation, subject line, and closing. You can see a live preview as you type.

Common Uses

This generator is useful for fee concession requests, transfer certificate applications, bonafide certificate requests, leave applications, duplicate marksheet requests, character certificate applications, and general correspondence with the principal.

Formal Letter Format

The application follows the standard formal letter format: sender details, date, recipient address (The Principal), subject line, salutation (Respected Sir/Madam), body paragraphs, and a formal closing with the student's name and class.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of applications can I generate?
You can generate any formal application addressed to a principal, including fee concession, leave, transfer certificate, bonafide certificate, and general requests.
Can I customize the body text?
Yes, the tool provides a body text field where you can write your own content or use the placeholder as a guide.
Is the format accepted by schools?
The generated application uses the standard formal letter format accepted by schools and colleges across India.
Do I need to create an account?
No account is needed. The tool is completely free and works entirely in your browser.
