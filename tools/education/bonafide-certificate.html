What is a Bonafide Certificate?

A bonafide certificate is an official document issued by an educational institution such as a school, college, or university. It certifies that a particular student is enrolled in a specific course or program during a given academic year. This certificate is widely used as proof of student identity and enrollment status for various administrative and legal purposes.

When Do You Need a Bonafide Certificate?

Students may need a bonafide certificate for many reasons, including applying for scholarships, opening a bank account, applying for a passport or visa, seeking admission to another institution, applying for internships, or claiming student discounts on transportation and services. Many government and private organizations require this document as part of their verification process.
