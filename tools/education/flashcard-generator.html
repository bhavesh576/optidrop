Create and Study Flashcards Online

Our flashcard generator lets you create digital flashcards for any subject. Flashcards are one of the most effective study tools because they leverage active recall, a learning technique where you actively stimulate your memory during the learning process.

How to Create Flashcards

Switch to Create Cards mode, name your flashcard set, and start adding cards. Each card has a front (the question or term) and a back (the answer or definition). Add as many cards as you need and save the set for later study sessions.

Studying with Flashcards

In Study mode, click any card to flip it and reveal the answer. After reviewing each card, mark it as "Know It" or "Don't Know". At the end of the session, you can review only the cards you marked as unknown for focused practice.

The Science Behind Flashcards

Flashcards work through two key learning principles: active recall (forcing your brain to retrieve information) and spaced repetition (reviewing material at increasing intervals). By marking cards as known or unknown, you naturally implement a form of spaced repetition.

Tips for Effective Flashcards

Keep each card focused on a single concept. Use simple, clear language. Include examples where helpful. Review your cards regularly rather than cramming all at once. Shuffle the order to avoid memorizing the sequence rather than the content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create flashcards?
Switch to Create mode, give your set a name, and add cards by entering text for the front and back of each card. Click Save Set when done.
How does the study mode work?
In Study mode, click a card to flip it and reveal the answer. Mark each card as 'Known' or 'Unknown' to track your progress. Cards marked unknown will be shown again.
Where are my flashcard sets saved?
Flashcard sets are saved in your browser's localStorage. They persist between sessions without needing an account. Data stays entirely on your device.
Can I study only the cards I don't know?
Yes, after an initial pass through all cards, you can choose to review only the cards you marked as unknown for focused practice.
