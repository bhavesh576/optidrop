How to Use the GPA Calculator

Our free GPA calculator makes it easy to compute your Grade Point Average in seconds. Simply add your subjects, enter the credit hours for each course, and select your letter grade from the dropdown menu.

Understanding GPA Calculation

GPA is calculated using a weighted average formula: multiply each course's grade points by its credit hours to get quality points, then divide the total quality points by the total credit hours. This ensures courses with more credit hours have a proportionally larger impact on your GPA.

Grade Point Scale

On the standard 4.0 scale, an A+ equals 4.0 grade points, a B+ equals 3.3, and so on down to F which equals 0.0. Our calculator also supports a 10-point scale commonly used in many international universities.

GPA vs CGPA

GPA typically refers to your grade point average for a single semester, while CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) represents your overall average across all semesters. You can use this calculator for either by simply entering all your courses.

Tips for Improving Your GPA

Focus on courses with higher credit hours since they have more weight. Prioritize studying for subjects where you're closest to a grade boundary. Even a small improvement in a high-credit course can significantly boost your overall GPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GPA calculated?
GPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade points by its credit hours, summing those products, and dividing by the total number of credit hours.
What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?
GPA typically refers to a single semester's grade point average, while CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the average across all semesters combined.
What is a good GPA?
On a 4.0 scale, a GPA of 3.5 or above is generally considered good. A GPA of 3.7+ is excellent and may qualify for honors or dean's list.
Can I calculate weighted GPA with this tool?
Yes, this calculator supports weighted GPA. Simply enter the correct credit hours for each course, and the tool will calculate the weighted average automatically.
