Free Sick Leave Application Generator for Students

Writing a sick leave application is a common requirement for students in schools and colleges. Our generator creates a properly formatted formal leave application that follows the standard letter format used in educational institutions across India.

How to Use This Tool

Simply fill in your name, class, institution, the dates you need leave for, the reason for your illness, and your parent or guardian's name. The tool will generate a complete leave application with a live preview. Once satisfied, click the Download PDF button to save a professional copy.

Standard Format Used

The generated application follows the standard formal letter format: it includes the sender's details, date, recipient (The Principal), subject line, body with the leave request details, and a formal closing with the parent's name and signature line.

Tips for a Good Leave Application

Be specific about the dates of leave requested. Mention the illness or medical reason clearly. If you have a medical certificate, mention it in the application. Keep the tone polite and formal. Always have your parent or guardian sign the application before submitting.
