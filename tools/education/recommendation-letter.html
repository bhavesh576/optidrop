Free Recommendation Letter Generator

A strong recommendation letter can significantly boost a student's application for university admissions, scholarships, or jobs. This tool helps teachers and professors draft professional recommendation letters quickly and efficiently.

What Makes a Good Recommendation

The best recommendation letters include specific examples of the student's abilities, not just generic praise. They mention concrete achievements, describe the context of the relationship, and explain why the student is suited for the specific opportunity they are pursuing.

How to Use This Tool

Enter the student's name and institution, the recommender's name and designation, the relationship context, key qualities to highlight, and the purpose of the recommendation. The tool generates a professionally formatted letter that you can customize before downloading.

Customization Tips

After generating the letter, consider adding specific anecdotes or examples that demonstrate the student's qualities. Mention particular projects, presentations, or achievements that only someone who knows the student well would know about.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a recommendation letter?
A recommendation letter is a formal document written by a teacher, professor, or employer that vouches for a student's character, abilities, and achievements.
Who should write a recommendation letter?
Recommendation letters are best written by professors, teachers, or supervisors who know the student well and can speak to their academic performance, character, and potential.
How long should a recommendation letter be?
A good recommendation letter is typically one page long (300-500 words). It should be concise but include specific examples of the student's qualities and achievements.
Can I edit the generated letter?
Yes, you can customize all fields before downloading. The generated letter serves as a professional template that you can further refine.
