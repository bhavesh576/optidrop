Free Currency Trip Converter — Track Multi-Currency Expenses

OptiDrop's Currency Trip Converter is the perfect tool for tracking expenses during international travel. Add expenses in any currency — euros, pounds, yen, rupees, or any of 30+ supported currencies — and the tool automatically converts everything to your home currency for easy tracking.

How to Use the Currency Trip Converter

First, select your home currency from the dropdown. Then, for each expense, enter the amount, select the currency it was spent in, choose a category, and add an optional description. The tool shows your total spending, category breakdown, and a detailed expense log. All data is saved in your browser.

Why Track Travel Expenses?

Tracking expenses while traveling helps you stay within budget, understand spending patterns, and plan better for future trips. When visiting multiple countries with different currencies, having a unified view of all expenses in your home currency is invaluable for financial planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our currency trip converter lets you enter expenses in any supported currency. Each expense is automatically converted to your chosen home currency using built-in exchange rates. You can add a description, category, and amount for each expense, and the tool shows totals by category and overall.
Our tool uses static exchange rates that are updated periodically. These rates are approximate and intended for budget planning purposes. Actual rates at the time of your transaction may differ slightly. For precise conversions, check with your bank or a live currency converter.
Common travel expense categories include: Food & Dining, Transportation, Accommodation, Activities & Entertainment, Shopping, and Miscellaneous. Categorizing expenses helps you understand where your money goes and identify areas where you can save on future trips.
Currently, all expense data is stored locally in your browser. You can view, edit, and delete expenses within the tool. The data persists across browser sessions using local storage. For record-keeping, you can take screenshots of the summary view or manually note down your totals.
