Free Trip Cost Estimator — Plan Your Travel Budget Online

OptiDrop's Trip Cost Estimator is a free online tool that helps you plan your travel budget before you leave. Whether you are planning a weekend getaway or a month-long adventure, this tool lets you estimate costs across five key categories: accommodation, food and drinks, transportation, activities, and miscellaneous expenses.

How to Use the Trip Cost Estimator

Enter your destination name, the number of days you plan to travel, and your estimated daily budget for each category. The tool instantly calculates your total trip cost, daily average, and shows a visual breakdown. You can also save multiple trip plans to compare destinations or travel styles.

Why Plan Your Travel Budget?

Planning your travel budget in advance helps you avoid overspending, ensures you have enough funds for the entire trip, and allows you to prioritize experiences that matter most to you. All calculations happen in your browser — no data is uploaded or stored on any server.
