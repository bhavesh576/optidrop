Free Itinerary Planner — Organize Your Travel Schedule

OptiDrop's Itinerary Planner helps you organize your trip with a structured day-by-day schedule. For each day, you can add multiple activities with specific times, locations, and notes. The timeline view gives you a clear overview of your entire trip at a glance.

How to Use the Itinerary Planner

Enter a trip name, then click "Add Day" to create your first day. For each day, click "Add Activity" to add activities with a time, location, and optional notes. Drag activities to reorder them, or delete activities you no longer need. Save your itinerary to access it later.

Benefits of Planning an Itinerary

A well-planned itinerary helps you make the most of your travel time, ensures you do not miss must-see attractions, and reduces decision fatigue during your trip. It also helps with budgeting and booking reservations in advance. All data stays in your browser for complete privacy.
