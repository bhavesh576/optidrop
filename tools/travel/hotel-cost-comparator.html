How to Use the Hotel Cost Comparator

Enter the number of nights you plan to stay, then fill in hotel names and their nightly rates for each option you are considering. Click "Compare Hotels" to see a visual comparison with total costs, cost per night, and the cheapest option automatically highlighted with potential savings.

Tips for Finding the Best Hotel Rates

Compare prices across multiple booking platforms, check the hotel's direct website for exclusive deals, consider booking during off-peak seasons, look for package deals that include breakfast or parking, and always read recent reviews. Remember that the cheapest option may not always be the best value when considering location, amenities, and service quality.

Nightly rates are just one factor. Also consider: taxes and resort fees (often 10-20% extra), location and transportation costs to attractions, included amenities (breakfast, WiFi, parking), cancellation policies, and loyalty program benefits. The cheapest nightly rate may not always be the best value overall.
Generally, booking 2-3 months in advance for popular destinations gives good rates. For business hotels, booking on weekends often gets better prices. Use price comparison tools, check for last-minute deals, and consider traveling during shoulder season for the best rates.
Hotel prices displayed online often do not include taxes and fees. These can add 10-30% to the base rate depending on the location. Some cities also charge tourist taxes or resort fees. When comparing hotels, make sure to compare the total cost including all taxes and fees for an accurate comparison.
