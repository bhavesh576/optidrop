About the Feeding Schedule Tracker

Keeping track of your baby's feeding schedule is important for ensuring they're getting enough nutrition. Our tracker makes it easy to log every feeding session with time, type, amount, and notes.

Why Track Feedings?

  • Nutrition Monitoring - Ensure your baby is getting enough milk or food
  • Pattern Recognition - Identify feeding patterns and adjust schedules
  • Doctor Visits - Provide accurate feeding information to pediatricians
  • Parent Communication - Share feeding logs between caregivers

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of feedings can I track?
You can track three types of feedings: breastfeeding (with left/right side), bottle feeding (with amount in ml/oz), and solid food (with notes about what was eaten).
Is my feeding data saved?
Yes! All feeding data is saved in your browser's localStorage. It persists between sessions but is only stored on your device. No data is sent to any server.
Can I see today's feeding summary?
Yes! The tool shows a summary of today's feedings including total count, types breakdown, and total amount for bottle feedings. You can also view your full feeding history.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. All data is stored locally on your device using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server, making it 100% private and secure.
