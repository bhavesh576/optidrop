About the Milestone Tracker

Developmental milestones are skills or activities that most children can do by a certain age. Tracking these milestones helps parents understand their child's development and identify any areas that may need attention.

Types of Development

  • Physical/Motor - Gross motor (crawling, walking) and fine motor (grasping, drawing)
  • Language - Babbling, first words, sentences, and communication skills
  • Social/Emotional - Smiling, interacting with others, expressing emotions
  • Cognitive - Problem-solving, learning, understanding cause and effect

Frequently Asked Questions

What age ranges are covered?
The milestone tracker covers 5 age ranges: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-3 years, and 3-5 years. Each range includes age-appropriate developmental milestones.
What types of milestones are tracked?
Milestones are categorized into physical/motor skills, language/communication, social/emotional, and cognitive development. Each category has specific, age-appropriate milestones.
Is my progress saved?
Yes! All milestone progress is saved in your browser's localStorage. It persists between sessions but is only stored on your device. No data is sent to any server.
Should I be concerned if my child hasn't reached a milestone?
Every child develops at their own pace. These milestones are general guidelines, not strict timelines. If you have concerns about your child's development, consult your pediatrician.
