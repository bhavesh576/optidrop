About the Height Prediction Calculator

Our height prediction calculator uses the mid-parental height method, a well-established medical formula used by pediatricians worldwide. This method provides a reasonable estimate of a child's adult height based on genetic factors.

How the Formula Works

The mid-parental height formula calculates the average of both parents' heights, then adjusts based on the child's gender:

  • For Boys: (Father's height + Mother's height + 13) / 2
  • For Girls: (Father's height + Mother's height - 13) / 2

The predicted height typically falls within a range of plus or minus 4 inches (10 cm) of the calculated value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the height prediction?
The mid-parental height method provides an estimate within about 4 inches (10 cm) of actual adult height for most children. It's a well-established medical formula but is not a guarantee.
What is the mid-parental height method?
The mid-parental height method is a medical formula that estimates a child's adult height based on the average of both parents' heights, with adjustments for the child's gender.
Can I use metric or imperial units?
Yes! You can enter heights in either centimeters (cm) or feet and inches. The calculator will display results in both units.
