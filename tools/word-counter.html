Free Online Word Counter & Character Counter

OptiDrop's Word Counter is a free, browser-based tool that instantly counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and estimates reading time. Whether you are writing an essay, a blog post, a social media caption, or an academic paper, this tool gives you all the text statistics you need in real time. Simply paste or type your text and watch the counts update as you go.

Real-Time Text Statistics

All statistics update instantly as you type or edit your text. There is no "Calculate" button to press — the tool works in real time. The word count breaks text on whitespace boundaries, the character count includes and excludes spaces separately, sentences are detected by punctuation marks (. ! ?), and paragraphs are identified by blank lines.

Estimated Reading Time

The estimated reading time is calculated at 200 words per minute, which is the average reading speed for adults. This is useful for blog posts, presentations, speeches, and any content where knowing the reading duration helps with planning and delivery.
