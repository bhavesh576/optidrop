Frequently Asked Questions

BAC is the percentage of alcohol in your bloodstream. A BAC of 0.08% means 0.08 grams of alcohol per 100 milliliters of blood. In most countries, 0.08% is the legal limit for driving.
The Widmark formula calculates BAC as: BAC = (Alcohol in grams / (Body weight in grams x Widmark ratio)) x 100 - (Elimination rate x hours). The ratio is 0.68 for men and 0.55 for women.
The body eliminates alcohol at about 0.015% BAC per hour. It takes roughly one hour to process one standard drink. Coffee and cold showers do not speed up elimination — only time works.
This calculator provides estimates based on the Widmark formula. Actual BAC varies by metabolism, food intake, medications, and health conditions. Never rely solely on a calculator to determine if you are safe to drive.
