Free Medication Reminder — Never Miss a Dose

Keep track of your medications with our free online pill tracker. Add each medication with its name, dosage, frequency, and time. View your daily schedule and check off doses as you take them.

How It Works

Enter your medication details in the form above and click Add Medication. Your medications appear in the Today's Schedule section. Check the box when you take a dose. All data is saved in your browser's localStorage, so it persists between visits.

100% Private

Your medication data never leaves your browser. Everything is stored locally using localStorage. No accounts, no servers, no data collection.
