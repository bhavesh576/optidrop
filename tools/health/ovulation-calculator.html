Understanding Your Fertile Window

Your fertile window spans 6 days: the 5 days before ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, while the egg survives for only 12-24 hours after being released. Having intercourse during the 2-3 days leading up to ovulation and on ovulation day provides the highest chance of conception.
