Frequently Asked Questions

Multiply patient weight (kg) by prescribed dose per kg to get total dose. Then divide by medicine concentration to get the volume to administer. For example: 70 kg x 5 mg/kg = 350 mg. At 50 mg/ml concentration, that is 7 ml.
mg (milligrams) and mcg (micrograms) are weight units. 1 mg = 1000 mcg. ml (milliliters) is a volume unit. Medicine concentration is weight per volume (e.g., 250 mg/5 ml). Divide dose in mg by concentration to get volume in ml.
Weight-based dosing ensures each patient gets the right amount based on body size. It is critical for children, elderly patients, and drugs with a narrow therapeutic window. Under-dosing may be ineffective; over-dosing can cause adverse effects.
