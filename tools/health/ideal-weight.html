Understanding the Four Formulas

Each formula was developed independently by medical researchers. The Devine formula (1974) is the most commonly used, originally designed for drug dosage calculations. The Robinson formula (1983) was developed as a refinement of Devine's work. The Miller formula (1983) provides slightly different estimates. The Hamwi formula (1964) is one of the oldest methods, often used in clinical settings. By comparing all four, you get a more reliable weight range than any single formula provides.
