Free Water Intake Calculator — Know Your Daily Water Needs

Staying properly hydrated is essential for your health. Water supports digestion, nutrient absorption, temperature regulation, joint lubrication, and organ function. OptiDrop's Water Intake Calculator provides a personalized daily water recommendation based on your body weight, physical activity level, and the climate you live in.

How the Calculator Works

The base calculation uses the standard recommendation of 30-35 ml of water per kg of body weight per day. This is then adjusted upward for higher activity levels (to account for sweat loss) and for hot climates (where you lose more water through perspiration). Cold climates may slightly reduce the baseline. The result is shown in litres, glasses (250 ml each), and ounces for convenience.

Hydration Tracker

After calculating your recommended intake, use the hydration tracker to log each glass of water you drink throughout the day. Simply click on a glass icon to mark it as consumed. Your progress is saved to your browser's local storage, so you can return later and continue tracking. Reset the tracker at the start of each new day.
