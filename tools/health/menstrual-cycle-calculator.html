Frequently Asked Questions

Your next period is calculated by adding your cycle length to the first day of your last period. For a 28-day cycle starting May 1, your next period is expected on May 29. This assumes a regular cycle.
The fertile window is about 6 days: 5 days before ovulation and the day of ovulation. For a 28-day cycle, ovulation is around day 14, making the fertile window roughly days 9-14.
Ovulation is when a mature egg is released from the ovary, typically around day 14 of a 28-day cycle. The egg survives 12-24 hours, but sperm can survive up to 5 days, creating a fertile window of about 6 days.
These are estimates based on regular cycles. Actual lengths vary due to stress, illness, travel, and other factors. This calculator should not be used as a sole method of contraception or fertility planning.
