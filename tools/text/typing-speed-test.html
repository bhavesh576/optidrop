About Typing Speed Tests

A typing speed test measures how quickly and accurately you can type a given passage of text. The standard unit of measurement is WPM (Words Per Minute), where one "word" is defined as five characters including spaces. This standardized measurement allows fair comparison across different texts and typing sessions.

The average person types at around 40 WPM, which is sufficient for everyday tasks. Professional typists and administrative assistants typically achieve 60 to 75 WPM, while competitive typists can exceed 100 WPM. Improving your typing speed can significantly boost productivity in any job that involves computer work, from writing emails to coding software.

Accuracy matters as much as speed. Typing quickly but making frequent errors often slows you down overall, since you spend time correcting mistakes. This tool measures both WPM and accuracy so you can focus on balanced improvement. A good approach is to first aim for 98% accuracy, then gradually increase your speed while maintaining that accuracy level.

This typing speed test runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to any server, making it 100% private. You get five different passages that rotate randomly, keeping each session fresh and challenging. Use it daily to track your progress and watch your WPM climb over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

The average typing speed is around 40 words per minute (WPM). A good typing speed is considered 50-60 WPM, while professional typists and data entry workers often reach 65-75 WPM. Expert typists can exceed 100 WPM. Most office jobs require a minimum of 40 WPM. Regular practice with typing tests can help you improve over time.
WPM (Words Per Minute) is calculated by counting the number of correctly typed words and dividing by the elapsed time in minutes. A word is standardized as 5 characters including spaces. This tool counts only correctly typed words toward your WPM score. Accuracy is calculated as the percentage of correctly typed characters out of total characters typed.
To improve typing speed: practice regularly with timed typing tests, learn proper finger placement on the home row, avoid looking at the keyboard, focus on accuracy first and speed will follow, maintain good posture, and take breaks to avoid fatigue. Consistent daily practice of 15-30 minutes can significantly improve your speed within a few weeks.
