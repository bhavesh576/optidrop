Free Online Reading Time Calculator

OptiDrop's Reading Time Calculator is a free, browser-based tool that instantly calculates how long it takes to read or speak any text. Simply paste or type your content and get real-time statistics including word count, character count (with and without spaces), sentence count, paragraph count, estimated reading time at 200 words per minute, and estimated speaking time at 150 words per minute.

Why Calculate Reading Time?

Knowing the reading time of your content is essential for bloggers, content creators, students, presenters, and public speakers. Blog readers want to know how long an article will take before committing. Presenters need to time their speeches. Students need to estimate study time for reading assignments. This tool gives you all the metrics you need in one place.

Reading Speed vs. Speaking Speed

The average adult reads at approximately 200 words per minute (WPM), while the average speaking rate is about 150 WPM. This means a 1,000-word text takes roughly 5 minutes to read but about 6 minutes and 40 seconds to speak aloud. These benchmarks are widely used by content platforms, publishers, and speech coaches.
