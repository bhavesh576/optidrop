    Frequently Asked Questions

    A URL slug is the part of a URL that identifies a specific page in human-readable form. For example, in "example.com/blog/my-first-post", the slug is "my-first-post". A good slug is lowercase, uses hyphens instead of spaces, removes special characters, and describes the page content clearly for both users and search engines.
    An SEO-friendly URL should be short, descriptive, lowercase, use hyphens to separate words, and include relevant keywords. Avoid special characters, underscores (use hyphens instead), and stop words like "a", "the", "and" when possible. Our slug generator handles all of this automatically when you paste your title or text.

    URLs can contain letters (a-z, A-Z), digits (0-9), hyphens (-), underscores (_), tildes (~), and periods (.). Special characters like spaces, quotes, brackets, and accented characters should be avoided or encoded.
