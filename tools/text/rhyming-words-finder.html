What Are Rhyming Words?

Rhyming words are words that have similar ending sounds, typically from the last stressed vowel onward. For example, "cat" rhymes with "hat", "bat", and "mat". Rhymes are fundamental to poetry, songwriting, and many forms of creative writing.

Types of Rhymes

  • Perfect Rhyme - Words with identical ending sounds (cat/hat, love/dove)
  • Slant Rhyme - Words with similar but not identical sounds (cat/cut, love/move)
  • Eye Rhyme - Words that look similar but don't sound alike (love/move)
  • Internal Rhyme - Rhymes within a single line of verse
