Frequently Asked Questions

Word frequency analysis counts how many times each word appears in a text. It helps identify the most common terms, detect writing patterns, analyze keyword density for SEO, and understand the vocabulary distribution of a document. Writers use it to avoid overusing certain words.
Paste your text into this tool and click Analyze. The tool splits the text into individual words, normalizes them to lowercase, counts each occurrence, and displays the top 20 most frequent words ranked by count and percentage. You can toggle stop word filtering to see more meaningful results.
Stop words are extremely common words that usually carry little meaning on their own, such as "the", "a", "is", "are", "and", "but", "in", "on", "at", "to", "for". They are often filtered out in text analysis to focus on more meaningful content words. This tool has a built-in list of 150+ common English stop words.
Simply paste your text into the input area and click the Analyze button. This tool instantly provides total word count, unique word count, and a frequency table of the most common words. You can copy the frequency table as text for use in reports or further analysis.
