About the Letter Count Analyzer

Our letter count analyzer provides detailed character frequency analysis of any text. It counts every character type and displays a visual bar chart showing how often each letter from A to Z appears in your text.

Understanding Letter Frequency

In English text, the most common letters are typically E, T, A, O, I, N, S, H, and R. This pattern is the basis for frequency analysis, a technique used in cryptography to break ciphers. The letter E alone accounts for about 12-13% of all letters in English text.

Uses for Letter Frequency Analysis

  • Cryptography - Breaking simple substitution ciphers
  • Language Learning - Understanding letter patterns in different languages
  • Writing Analysis - Checking for overuse of certain letters
  • Data Entry - Verifying text accuracy and completeness

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the letter count analyzer show?
The analyzer shows total characters, letters, digits, spaces, and punctuation counts. It also displays a visual bar chart showing the frequency of each letter (A-Z) and identifies the most and least common letters.
How is letter frequency calculated?
Letter frequency is calculated by counting how many times each letter (A-Z) appears in your text, ignoring case. The bar chart visually represents these counts so you can quickly see which letters appear most often.
Can I analyze large texts?
Yes! The analyzer works with texts of any size. For very large texts (over 10,000 characters), the analysis may take a moment but will still complete entirely in your browser.
Is my text data stored or shared?
No. All text processing happens entirely in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server, never stored anywhere, and never leaves your device. 100% private.
