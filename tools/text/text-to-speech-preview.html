About Text to Speech

This tool uses the Web Speech API built into modern browsers to convert text to speech. No external services or downloads are required. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Features

  • Multiple voices: Choose from all voices available in your browser and OS
  • Adjustable rate: Speed up or slow down speech from 0.5x to 2x
  • Pitch control: Change the pitch of the voice
  • Volume control: Adjust the output volume
  • Playback controls: Speak, pause/resume, and stop buttons

Browser Support

The Web Speech API is supported in all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Voice availability may vary by browser and operating system.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work offline?
Yes, the text-to-speech functionality uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API. Once the page is loaded, it works without an internet connection.
How many voices are available?
The number of voices depends on your browser and operating system. Most modern browsers offer 20-50+ voices across different languages.
Can I adjust the speech speed?
Yes, you can adjust the speech rate from 0.5x (half speed) to 2x (double speed), as well as the pitch and volume using the sliders.
Is my text data stored or shared?
No. All text processing happens entirely in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server, never stored anywhere, and never leaves your device. This makes it safe for sensitive documents and personal content.
