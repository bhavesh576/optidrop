Look at the scrambled letters and figure out the hidden word. Type your guess and click Submit. If you are stuck, click Hint to see the first letter. Click Skip to move to the next word. Earn 10 points per correct answer (5 with hint, 0 for skip). Challenge yourself to get the highest score!
