Free Magic 8-Ball Online — Ask Yes or No Questions

OptiDrop's Yes or No Decider is a fun online Magic 8-Ball simulator. Type any yes or no question, click Shake, and watch the 8-Ball animate before revealing your answer. With 20 classic responses including positive, negative, and neutral answers, every shake brings a new surprise.

20 Classic Magic 8-Ball Responses

The answers include 10 positive responses like "Yes - Definitely" and "Without a doubt," 5 negative responses like "No" and "Don't count on it," and 5 neutral responses like "Ask again later" and "Cannot predict now." Each response is randomly selected with equal probability.

Track Your Question History

Every question you ask and its answer is saved in the session history. Review your last 10 questions and their answers. This is a fun way to keep track of the decisions the Magic 8-Ball has made for you during your session.
