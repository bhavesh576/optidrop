Free Online Dice Roller — Roll Virtual Dice Instantly

OptiDrop's Dice Roller lets you roll up to 10 dice at once with animated visuals. Choose from six common dice types: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, and d20. Each die is color-coded by type and animates with a shaking effect before revealing the result. The tool shows individual die values, the total sum, highest, and lowest roll.

Perfect for Board Games and RPGs

Whether you are playing Dungeons and Dragons, Yahtzee, or any tabletop game, this dice roller has you covered. Roll damage dice, skill checks, or initiative rolls with a single click. The d20 roller is especially popular for D&D players who need quick and fair random outcomes.

Roll History and Statistics

Every roll is recorded in your session history so you can review past results. See the total, highest, and lowest values for each roll. The history stores your last 10 rolls and can be cleared at any time with the Reset button.
