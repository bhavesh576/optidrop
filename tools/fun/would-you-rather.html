Would You Rather Game — The Ultimate Conversation Starter

Would You Rather is one of the most popular party games in the world. The concept is simple: you are presented with two options and must choose one. Both options are designed to be equally appealing or equally challenging, making every decision a fun dilemma.

How to Play

Simply click on the option you prefer. After you choose, see what percentage of people picked each option. Then click "Next Question" for a brand new dilemma. With over 50 unique questions, you will never run out of fun scenarios to debate.

Perfect for Groups

Whether you are at a party, on a road trip, or just hanging out with friends, Would You Rather is the perfect game to spark hilarious debates and surprising revelations. Take turns answering and compare your choices for maximum fun.
