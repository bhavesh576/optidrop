Multiple Game Modes

Play in Free Play mode for unlimited rounds, or challenge yourself with Best of 5, Best of 10, or Best of 20 modes. In best-of modes, the game tracks progress and declares a match winner when one side reaches the majority. A progress bar shows the match status at a glance.

Score Tracking and History

Your wins, losses, and draws are tracked across all rounds. The round history shows your last 10 games with each player's choice and the result. Reset your score at any time to start fresh. The computer's choices are truly random, so each game is fair and unpredictable.
