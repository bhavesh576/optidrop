Looking for color inspiration? OptiDrop's Random Color Generator creates beautiful random colors with one click. Each color is displayed in hex, RGB, and HSL formats, making it easy to use in any design project. Simply click the Generate button or press the spacebar to discover a new color.

Three Color Formats

Every generated color is shown in three formats: Hex (e.g., #FF5733) for CSS and web design, RGB (e.g., rgb(255, 87, 51)) for digital displays, and HSL (e.g., hsl(14, 100%, 60%)) for intuitive color manipulation. Click any value card to copy it to your clipboard instantly.

Color History

The tool keeps track of your last 10 generated colors in a visual history bar. Click on any color in the history to revisit it. This is perfect for comparing colors or building a palette from your favorite random picks.
