How Percentage Calculations Work

A percentage represents a fraction of 100. To find X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. To find what percent X is of Y, divide X by Y and multiply by 100. For percentage change, subtract the original from the new value, divide by the original, and multiply by 100. Our calculator applies these formulas instantly so you never have to compute manually.

Frequently Asked Questions

To calculate a percentage, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, to find what percentage 25 is of 200, compute (25 / 200) x 100 = 12.5%. Our percentage calculator does this instantly for you.
To find X percent of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = (20 / 100) x 150 = 30. Use the "What is X% of Y?" tab in our calculator for instant results.
Percentage increase is calculated by subtracting the original value from the new value, dividing by the original value, and multiplying by 100. Formula: ((New - Original) / Original) x 100. For example, going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.
Percentage decrease is calculated by subtracting the new value from the original value, dividing by the original value, and multiplying by 100. Formula: ((Original - New) / Original) x 100. For example, going from 100 to 75 is a 25% decrease.
