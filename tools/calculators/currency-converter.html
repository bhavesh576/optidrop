Frequently Asked Questions

To convert one currency to another, multiply the amount by the exchange rate between the two currencies. For example, to convert 100 USD to INR at an exchange rate of 83.50: 100 x 83.50 = 8,350 INR. Our converter does this automatically using pre-loaded exchange rates. Just enter the amount, select the source and target currencies, and see the result instantly.
An exchange rate is the value of one currency expressed in terms of another currency. For example, if the USD to INR exchange rate is 83.50, it means 1 US dollar is worth 83.50 Indian rupees. Exchange rates fluctuate constantly based on supply and demand in the foreign exchange market, economic conditions, interest rates, inflation, and geopolitical events.
Exchange rates change due to several factors: supply and demand in the foreign exchange market, differences in inflation rates between countries, differences in interest rates, trade balances (exports vs imports), political stability and economic performance, government debt levels, and speculation by traders. Currencies of countries with strong economies and low inflation tend to appreciate over time.
To convert USD to INR, multiply the dollar amount by the current USD/INR exchange rate. For example, if the rate is 83.50: $100 = 100 x 83.50 = Rs. 8,350. You can check live rates on financial websites, bank websites, or use our converter above. Note that banks and money changers typically add a margin to the exchange rate, so the actual rate you receive may be slightly different.
