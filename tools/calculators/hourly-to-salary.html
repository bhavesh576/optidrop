How to Convert Hourly Rate to Salary

Multiply your hourly rate by hours worked per week, then by weeks worked per year. For example, $25/hour × 40 hours/week × 52 weeks/year = $52,000/year. This calculator also shows intermediate values like daily, weekly, biweekly, and monthly pay for easy comparison.

Standard Working Hours

A standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week for 52 weeks per year (2,080 hours total). If you take unpaid vacation, adjust the weeks per year accordingly. For example, 2 weeks unpaid = 50 weeks = 2,000 hours per year. The default values are set to the standard full-time schedule.
