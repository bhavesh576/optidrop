Free SIP Calculator Online — Calculate Mutual Fund Returns

OptiDrop's SIP Calculator is a free online tool that helps you calculate the returns on your Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) in mutual funds. Whether you are a beginner investor or an experienced one, this calculator gives you an accurate estimate of how your monthly investments will grow over time through the power of compounding.

How Does the SIP Calculator Work?

The calculator uses the future value of annuity formula to compute your SIP returns. Simply enter your monthly investment amount, expected annual rate of return, and the investment period in years. The tool instantly calculates your total invested amount, estimated returns, and the total maturity value. A visual breakdown bar shows the proportion of your investment versus the returns earned.

Why Use SIP for Investing?

SIP is one of the most disciplined ways to invest in mutual funds. It offers rupee cost averaging, which means you buy more units when prices are low and fewer units when prices are high. Over the long term, this averaging effect combined with the power of compounding can help you build significant wealth. Frequently Asked Questions

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly (usually monthly) in mutual funds. SIP allows you to build wealth over time through the power of compounding and rupee cost averaging, making it one of the most popular investment strategies in India.
SIP returns are calculated using the future value of annuity formula: FV = P x [(1 + r)^n - 1] / r x (1 + r), where P is the monthly investment amount, r is the monthly rate of return (annual rate divided by 12), and n is the total number of monthly installments. This accounts for compounding on each monthly contribution.
Historically, equity mutual funds in India have delivered average returns of 12-15% per annum over long periods (10+ years). Debt funds typically offer 6-8% returns. A good SIP return rate depends on your risk tolerance and investment horizon. For long-term wealth creation, equity SIPs with 12-15% expected returns are commonly recommended.
