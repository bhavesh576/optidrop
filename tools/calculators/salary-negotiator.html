Free Salary Negotiator Calculator — See the Real Impact of a Raise

OptiDrop's Salary Negotiator Calculator is a free online tool that helps you understand the full financial impact of a salary increase. Whether you are preparing for a performance review, considering a job offer, or planning to negotiate a raise, this calculator shows you exactly how much more you will earn monthly, annually, and over 5 years.

Why Calculate Before Negotiating?

Going into a salary negotiation with concrete numbers gives you confidence and credibility. Instead of asking for "a raise," you can say exactly how much you want and why. This calculator lets you compare different raise scenarios -- percentage-based vs. fixed amount -- and see the after-tax impact so you know your real take-home increase.

5-Year Salary Projection

The 5-year projection shows how a single raise compounds over time, especially if you receive similar increases each year. This long-term view helps you understand that even a small percentage raise today can translate to significant earnings over your career. All calculations happen in your browser with no data uploaded anywhere.

A raise increases your gross income, which means you will pay more in taxes. The additional income is taxed at your marginal tax rate, not your average rate. For example, if you are in the 22% tax bracket, each additional dollar of income results in roughly $0.78 after federal taxes. This calculator estimates the after-tax impact so you can see your real take-home increase.
A reasonable raise depends on your industry, experience, performance, and market conditions. Annual cost-of-living raises are typically 2-4%. Merit-based raises range from 5-15%.
