Calculate the percentage change between two values instantly. Useful for tracking price changes, salary increases, test score improvements, and more. The tool also includes a reverse calculator to find what X% of any number is.

The Formula

Percentage Change = ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) x 100. A positive result means an increase, and a negative result means a decrease.
