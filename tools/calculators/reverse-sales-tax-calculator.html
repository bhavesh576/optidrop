How Does Reverse Sales Tax Work?

The reverse sales tax formula divides the total amount by (1 + tax rate / 100). For example, if you paid $108 total and the tax rate was 8%, the pre-tax price is $108 / 1.08 = $100.00, and the tax was $8.00. This is the standard method used by accountants and bookkeepers to back-calculate pre-tax amounts.
