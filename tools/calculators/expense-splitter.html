Free Expense Splitter Online — Split Bills Easily

OptiDrop's Expense Splitter is a free online tool that helps you split bills and expenses among friends, roommates, or travel companions. Add your group members, enter each expense with who paid and who participated, and the tool calculates exactly who owes whom. Perfect for trips, dinners, shared households, and group activities.

How Does the Expense Splitter Work?

Start by adding the names of everyone in your group. Then enter each expense: the description, amount, who paid, and which members participated. The tool tracks each person's total paid and total share owed, then uses a debt simplification algorithm to determine the minimum number of payments needed to settle all balances.

Perfect for Group Trips

Planning a group trip? Add all your shared expenses as you go -- hotel, meals, transportation, activities. At the end of the trip, click Calculate Settlement to see the simplest way for everyone to settle up. Each expense can include different participants, so no one pays for things they did not join. All calculations happen in your browser with no data uploaded anywhere.
