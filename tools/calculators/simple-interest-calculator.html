Free Simple Interest Calculator Online

OptiDrop's Simple Interest Calculator helps you compute the interest earned or paid on a principal amount using the simple interest formula. Unlike compound interest, simple interest is calculated only on the original principal, making it straightforward and easy to understand.

How Does Simple Interest Work?

Simple interest uses the formula SI = (P × R × T) / 100, where P is the principal (initial amount), R is the annual interest rate, and T is the time in years. The total amount at maturity is the principal plus the interest. This calculator also supports time input in months, automatically converting to years.

Simple Interest vs Compound Interest

Simple interest is always calculated on the original principal only. Compound interest, on the other hand, is calculated on the principal plus accumulated interest. For the same rate and duration, compound interest yields more. Simple interest is commonly used for short-term loans, some bonds, and basic financial calculations.
