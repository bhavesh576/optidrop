Free Bill Split Calculator — Split Any Bill with Tip

OptiDrop's Bill Split Calculator makes it easy to divide a restaurant bill, group dinner, or any shared expense among friends, family, or coworkers. Enter the total bill, the number of people, and an optional tip percentage to instantly see how much each person owes.

How to Split a Bill Evenly

Enter the total bill amount before tip, the number of people splitting the bill, and the tip percentage you want to leave. The calculator shows the per-person amount both with and without tip, so everyone can see exactly what they owe. Quick tip buttons let you select common percentages like 10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, and 25%.

When to Use a Bill Splitter

This tool is perfect for restaurant dinners with friends, group travel expenses, shared household costs, office lunch orders, and any situation where multiple people share a cost. It handles the math instantly so you can focus on enjoying your meal.

Frequently Asked Questions

First, calculate the tip amount by multiplying the bill by the tip percentage. Add the tip to the bill to get the total. Then divide the total by the number of people. For example: $100 bill, 18% tip, 4 people. Tip = $18, Total = $118, Each person pays $29.50.
It's standard to calculate the tip on the total bill before splitting. This ensures everyone pays an equal share of the tip. This calculator follows that approach — tip is applied to the full bill, then the total is divided evenly.
In the United States, standard tip percentages are 15% for average service, 18% for good service, and 20% or more for excellent service. In many other countries, tipping is not expected or is lower (5-10%). This calculator lets you set any tip percentage.
This calculator splits the bill evenly among all people. For uneven splits (where some people ordered more), you would need to calculate each person's share of the subtotal separately, then divide the tip proportionally. For a quick even split, this tool is perfect.
