Frequently Asked Questions

Two-wheeler loan interest rates in India typically range from 8% to 16% per annum, depending on the lender, your credit score, loan amount, and tenure. Banks usually offer lower rates than NBFCs. Check with your bank for the current rate.
Most lenders offer two-wheeler loan tenures from 12 to 60 months (1-5 years). Some lenders may offer up to 84 months for premium bikes. Shorter tenures mean higher EMIs but less total interest paid.
