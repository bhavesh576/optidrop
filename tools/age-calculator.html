How Does the Age Calculator Work?

Simply enter your date of birth using the date picker and click "Calculate Age." The tool computes the difference between your birth date and today's date, accounting for varying month lengths and leap years. It then displays your age in multiple formats: years/months/days, total days, total hours, and total minutes.

Birthday Countdown

The calculator also tells you exactly how many days are left until your next birthday. Whether you are planning a celebration or just curious, this countdown helps you keep track. The tool works with any date — past or present — so you can also use it to find out the age of historical events, buildings, or anything else with a known start date.
