Frequently Asked Questions

Does the age calculator account for leap years?

Yes, the calculator fully accounts for leap years in its calculations. When computing the exact age in years, months, and days, it correctly handles varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and leap years that add an extra day to February. This ensures that the result is precise regardless of whether your birth year or any subsequent years were leap years. For example, if you were born on February 29 in a leap year, the calculator will correctly handle your age in non-leap years by using March 1 as your birthday equivalent.

Can I see my age in different units like weeks or hours?

Yes. In addition to the standard years, months, and days breakdown, the calculator displays your total days lived, total hours, and total minutes. These figures are calculated from the exact millisecond difference between your birth date and the current moment. For example, a 30-year-old has lived approximately 10,950 days, 262,800 hours, or 15.7 million minutes. These fun statistics help you appreciate just how much time has passed since you were born and can be interesting to share with friends and family.

Does the calculator show my zodiac sign?

The current version of the age calculator focuses on precise age computation and the birthday countdown rather than zodiac signs. However, once you know your birth date, you can easily determine your zodiac sign: Aries (March 21 - April 19), Taurus (April 20 - May 20), Gemini (May 21 - June 20), Cancer (June 21 - July 22), Leo (July 23 - August 22), Virgo (August 23 - September 22), Libra (September 23 - October 22), Scorpio (October 23 - November 21), Sagittarius (November 22 - December 21), Capricorn (December 22 - January 19), Aquarius (January 20 - February 18), or Pisces (February 19 - March 20).

Is my date of birth stored or shared with anyone?

Absolutely not. Your date of birth is processed entirely within your web browser and is never transmitted to any server, stored in any database, or shared with any third party. The moment you close the page or refresh it, your entered data is gone. OptiDrop tools are designed with a privacy-first approach — there are no accounts to create, no cookies that track your personal information, and no analytics that capture the dates you enter. You can use this tool with complete confidence that your personal data remains private.
