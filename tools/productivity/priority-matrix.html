Frequently Asked Questions

The Eisenhower Matrix is a productivity framework that organizes tasks into 4 quadrants based on urgency and importance: Do First (urgent+important), Schedule (important+not urgent), Delegate (urgent+not important), and Eliminate (neither).
Yes, you can drag and drop tasks between any of the 4 quadrants. This makes it easy to re-prioritize as circumstances change.
Yes, all tasks and their quadrant assignments are saved to your browser's localStorage. Your matrix persists between sessions.
Add all your tasks first, then sort them into quadrants. Do tasks in 'Do First' immediately, schedule 'Schedule' tasks, delegate 'Delegate' tasks if possible, and eliminate 'Eliminate' tasks.
