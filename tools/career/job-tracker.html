Job Application Tracker — Organize Your Job Search

Staying organized during a job search is critical to success. OptiDrop's Job Application Tracker lets you log every application, track its status through each stage, add notes for follow-ups, and see an overview of your entire job search at a glance. No spreadsheets to set up, no accounts to create, just start tracking.

Track Every Stage

Use the six status options to track where each application stands: Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer, Rejected, and Withdrawn. The status summary at the top gives you an instant count of applications in each stage, helping you understand your pipeline and focus your efforts.

Export and Back Up

Export your entire application history as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet tool. Since your data is stored locally in your browser, we recommend exporting regularly as a backup. Your data is 100% private and never leaves your device.
