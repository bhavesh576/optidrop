Frequently Asked Questions

A cover letter should be approximately 250 to 400 words, or about three to four paragraphs on a single page. Hiring managers spend only a few seconds scanning each cover letter, so keep it concise and focused on your most relevant qualifications and enthusiasm for the role.
A cover letter should include a professional greeting, a strong opening paragraph stating the position you are applying for and why you are interested, one or two body paragraphs highlighting your relevant skills and achievements with specific examples, and a closing paragraph with a call to action expressing your desire for an interview.
Address your cover letter to a specific person whenever possible, using 'Dear [Mr./Ms. Last Name].' If the hiring manager's name is not available, use 'Dear Hiring Manager' or 'Dear [Department] Team.' Avoid outdated salutations like 'To Whom It May Concern' as they feel impersonal and generic.
Yes, a cover letter should always be one page. Employers expect concise, focused communication. A one-page cover letter demonstrates your ability to communicate effectively and respects the hiring manager's time. Use clear paragraphs with adequate spacing for readability.
