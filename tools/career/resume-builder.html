Frequently Asked Questions

An ATS friendly resume is designed to pass through Applicant Tracking Systems used by employers to scan and filter resumes. It uses simple formatting, standard section headings, no graphics or tables, and clean text that automated systems can easily parse. The ATS Friendly template in this builder uses a straightforward layout optimized for these systems.
For most professionals, a resume should be one page. If you have more than 10 years of relevant experience, two pages is acceptable. Recent graduates and entry-level candidates should always aim for one page. Focus on the most relevant and recent experience that matches the job you are applying for.
A professional summary should be 2-3 sentences that highlight your years of experience, key skills, and career achievements. It should be tailored to each job application and include relevant keywords from the job description. Focus on what value you bring to the employer rather than what you want from the job.
List your skills in a dedicated section using comma-separated values or bullet points. Include both hard skills (technical abilities like programming languages, software proficiency) and soft skills (communication, leadership). Prioritize skills mentioned in the job description. Group related skills together and place the most relevant ones first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many resume templates are available and which should I choose?

There are four templates to choose from. The Classic template uses a traditional, clean layout suitable for most industries including finance, law, and government. The Modern template features blue accent headings and a contemporary design ideal for tech, marketing, and creative roles. The Minimal template uses understated formatting with subtle borders, perfect for designers and professionals who prefer a clean aesthetic. The ATS Friendly template uses Times New Roman font with simple formatting specifically optimized to pass through Applicant Tracking Systems used by large employers and recruitment agencies.

Will my resume pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)?

If you choose the "ATS Friendly" template, your resume is specifically designed to pass through automated screening systems. This template avoids graphics, tables, columns, and fancy formatting that confuse ATS software. It uses standard section headings like "Professional Summary," "Work Experience," "Education," and "Skills" that ATS systems are programmed to recognize. To further improve your ATS score, use keywords from the job description naturally in your summary and experience sections. The PDF output uses clean text that automated parsers can read without issues.

Can I add a photo to my resume?

The current version of the resume builder does not include a photo upload feature. This is intentional for several reasons. In many countries including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia, including a photo on a resume is discouraged or even prohibited due to anti-discrimination laws. In India and some European countries, photos are more common but not always required. By omitting the photo, the tool keeps the resume focused on your qualifications and experience, which is what most hiring managers care about. If you need a photo, you can add one after downloading the PDF using a PDF editor.

What sections are included in the resume and can I customize them?

The resume builder includes the following sections: Personal Information (name, email, phone, location, LinkedIn), Professional Summary, Work Experience (with multiple entries), Education (with multiple entries), Skills, and Certifications (optional). You can add multiple work experience and education entries using the "+ Add" buttons, and remove any entry using the X button. The Professional Summary has a 400-character limit to keep it concise. Skills are entered as comma-separated values and displayed as tags. All sections are included in the PDF output, and the builder will skip any section you leave empty so your resume stays clean.
