Post-Interview Thank You Letter Generator

Sending a thank you letter after an interview is one of the most effective ways to reinforce your candidacy. It shows professionalism, attention to detail, and genuine interest in the role. OptiDrop's Thank You Letter Generator creates a personalized letter that references specific topics from your interview, making it far more impactful than a generic template.

Make It Personal

The key to a great thank you letter is specificity. Mention something meaningful from your conversation, whether it was a project the team is working on, a challenge the company is facing, or a value you share. This demonstrates that you were actively listening and are genuinely engaged with the opportunity.

Send Within 24 Hours

Timing matters. Generate your thank you letter immediately after your interview while the details are fresh, and send it within 24 hours. Copy the text into an email or download as PDF for a more formal approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

You should send a thank you letter within 24 hours of your interview. Sending it the same day or the next morning shows promptness and genuine interest in the position. Email is the fastest and most common method, though a handwritten note can add a personal touch for more traditional industries.
A post-interview thank you letter should be concise, typically 150 to 250 words or about three to four short paragraphs. Hiring managers are busy, so get to the point quickly. Express gratitude, reference something specific from the interview, reaffirm your interest, and close professionally.
Include a genuine expression of thanks for the interviewer's time, a specific reference to something discussed during the interview to show you were engaged, a brief reinforcement of why you are a strong fit for the role, and a professional closing expressing your enthusiasm for the next steps.
While not always mandatory, sending a thank you letter is highly recommended and considered standard professional etiquette. It reinforces your interest in the position, keeps you top of mind with the interviewer, and demonstrates strong communication skills. Some hiring managers view the absence of a thank you note as a red flag.
