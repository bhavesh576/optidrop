Frequently Asked Questions

A cash voucher is an accounting document used to authorize and record cash payments. It contains details such as the voucher number, date, payee name, amount, purpose of payment, and the approval of authorized personnel. It serves as a record of the cash disbursement and is used for bookkeeping and audit purposes.
A cash voucher is an internal document used by a business to authorize and record a cash payment. A receipt is an external document given to a payee as proof that payment has been received. The voucher is for internal accounting; the receipt is for the payee's records.
Cash vouchers are typically approved by authorized personnel such as a department manager, finance manager, or business owner. The approval chain depends on the organization's internal controls and the amount being paid.
While not always legally mandated, cash vouchers are considered a best practice for business accounting. They provide an audit trail, help prevent fraud, ensure proper authorization of payments, and are essential for accurate bookkeeping.
