OptiDrop's Proforma Invoice Generator helps businesses create professional proforma invoices for advance orders, international trade, and cost estimates. A proforma invoice is a preliminary bill of sale sent before goods or services are delivered, giving the buyer a clear picture of the expected costs.

When to Use a Proforma Invoice

Proforma invoices are ideal for international shipments (customs use them for duty calculations), advance payment requests, budget approval processes, letter of credit transactions, and when you need to commit to pricing before finalizing the order.

Not a Tax Invoice

A proforma invoice is clearly marked as such and is not a legal demand for payment. It cannot be used for accounting or tax purposes. Once the order is confirmed and goods are delivered, a proper tax invoice should be issued. This tool clearly marks the document as "PROFORMA INVOICE" to avoid confusion.
