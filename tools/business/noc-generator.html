Frequently Asked Questions

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is an official document issued by an organization, institution, or individual stating that they have no objection to the details mentioned in the certificate. It is commonly required for travel abroad, changing jobs, pursuing higher education, property transactions, and various government and legal procedures.
A NOC is typically required when: applying for a visa or passport, traveling abroad while employed, transferring from one job to another, seeking admission to educational institutions while employed, registering a vehicle in another state, conducting property transactions, applying for a bank loan, or participating in government tenders. The specific requirement depends on the authority or institution requesting it.
A NOC is issued by the relevant authority depending on its purpose. Employers issue NOCs for employees seeking visas, travel, or job changes. Banks issue NOCs for loan closures or property transactions. Educational institutions issue NOCs for students transferring or seeking admission elsewhere. Government authorities issue NOCs for various legal and administrative purposes.
For government employees and certain public sector employees, a NOC from the employer is mandatory when applying for a passport. For private sector employees, a NOC is generally not required for passport application, but it may be required for the visa application process. Students may need a NOC from their institution. The specific requirements vary by country and applicant category.
