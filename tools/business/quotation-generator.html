Frequently Asked Questions

A business quotation is a document provided by a seller to a prospective buyer that outlines the prices, terms, and conditions for specific products or services. It details the items, quantities, unit prices, applicable taxes, total amount, validity period, and payment terms.
The validity of a quotation depends on the business and industry. Common validity periods are 7 days, 15 days, 30 days, or 60 days. It is important to clearly state the validity period on the quotation so the buyer knows how long the offered prices remain valid.
A quotation is a price estimate sent before a sale is made — it proposes prices and terms for a potential transaction. An invoice is sent after goods or services have been delivered, requesting payment. A quotation becomes an invoice once the buyer accepts the terms and the work is completed.
