OptiDrop's Delivery Challan Generator helps businesses create professional delivery challans (dispatch notes) in seconds. A delivery challan accompanies goods during transit and serves as proof that items have been dispatched from the seller to the buyer.

Track Your Shipments

Include vehicle numbers, transport modes, and detailed item lists to keep accurate records of all deliveries. The challan helps both the sender and receiver verify that the correct items and quantities have been shipped and received.

When to Use a Delivery Challan

Delivery challans are used when goods are transported for job work, on approval basis, for supply before the invoice is raised, or when goods are moved between warehouses. They are essential for maintaining proper documentation in supply chain operations.

Frequently Asked Questions
