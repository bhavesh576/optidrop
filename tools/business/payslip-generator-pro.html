Frequently Asked Questions

A professional payslip should include the employee name, ID, designation, department, pay period, and a detailed breakdown of earnings (basic pay, HRA, DA, other allowances) and deductions (PF, ESI, professional tax, other deductions). It should clearly show the gross salary, total deductions, and net take-home pay.
HRA (House Rent Allowance) is an allowance given by employers to employees for rental accommodation expenses. DA (Dearness Allowance) is a cost-of-living adjustment allowance paid to employees to offset the impact of inflation. Both are components of the gross salary and are common in Indian payroll systems.
Net salary (take-home pay) is calculated by subtracting total deductions from the gross salary. Net Salary = Gross Salary - (PF + ESI + Professional Tax + Other Deductions). Gross salary is the sum of basic pay, HRA, DA, and all other allowances before any deductions are made.
Gross salary is the total salary before any deductions. It includes basic pay, HRA, DA, and all other allowances. Net salary (also called take-home pay) is the amount the employee actually receives after all deductions like PF, ESI, professional tax, and income tax are subtracted from the gross salary.
