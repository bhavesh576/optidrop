Add Name & Date Stamp to Photos — Free, Private, No Upload

OptiDrop's Name & Date Stamper is a free online tool that lets you stamp your name and the current date directly onto any photo. This is especially useful for Indian exam forms like NEET, JEE, UPSC, SSC, IBPS, RRB, and other government applications that require you to write your name and date on the photograph before uploading. Instead of printing and manually writing on photos, you can do it digitally in seconds — at full resolution, with zero quality loss.

How the Name & Date Stamp Works

Simply upload your photo, enter your full name, select the date (today's date is pre-filled), choose where you want the stamp to appear (bottom-left, bottom-center, bottom-right, top-left, or top-right), pick a font size and text color, and click Stamp. The tool renders the text directly onto the image using the Canvas API — no server, no upload, no privacy risk. The final output is a high-quality JPG with your name and date burned in at the original image resolution.

Perfect for Exam Forms and Government Portals

Many competitive exam portals in India require candidates to affix their name and date on passport-size photographs. OptiDrop's stamper produces clean, legible stamps that meet these requirements. The output JPG is ready to upload directly to exam portals, government application forms, scholarship forms, and recruitment portals — all without printing, writing, or scanning.
