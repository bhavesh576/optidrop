Frequently Asked Questions

Can people scan this QR code without installing a special app?

Yes. Modern smartphones can scan QR codes directly through the default camera app — no third-party app installation is needed. On iPhones running iOS 11 or later, simply open the Camera app and point it at the QR code. On most Android phones, the Google Camera app or Google Lens recognizes QR codes automatically. The QR codes generated by OptiDrop follow the universal ISO/IEC 18004 standard, so they work with every QR code reader and scanner app available on any platform.

Can I customize the colors of my QR code?

Currently, OptiDrop generates standard black-on-white QR codes. This is a deliberate design choice because high-contrast black and white codes provide the best scan reliability across all devices, lighting conditions, and QR code readers. Custom-colored QR codes (such as dark blue on light gray) can reduce contrast and cause scanning failures, especially in low-light environments or with older phone cameras. If you need brand colors, you can add them to the area surrounding the QR code in your design software rather than modifying the code pattern itself.

How do I create a QR code for a WiFi network?

To create a WiFi QR code, select the Text type and enter your WiFi credentials in the standard format: WIFI:T:WPA;S:YourNetworkName;P:YourPassword;;. Replace WPA with WEP or leave it blank for open networks, replace YourNetworkName with your actual SSID, and replace YourPassword with your WiFi password. When someone scans this QR code, their phone will automatically prompt them to connect to your WiFi network without manually typing the password. This is perfect for coffee shops, offices, guest rooms, and homes.

Can I use QR codes generated here for commercial purposes?

Yes, you can use QR codes generated by OptiDrop for any personal or commercial purpose without restriction. There are no usage limits, no licensing fees, and no attribution requirements. You can print them on business cards, product packaging, marketing flyers, restaurant menus, event posters, merchandise, or use them in digital campaigns. The SVG download option is especially useful for commercial printing because it scales to any size — from a small label to a large banner — without losing sharpness. The codes never expire and will work as long as the encoded content remains valid.
