Privacy Matters for Students

Your academic work is personal. Whether it is a research paper, a resume with your contact details, or a scholarship application with your financial information, you should not have to upload it to a third-party server just to use a free tool.

  • Your resume data stays on your device. Resume content is processed and generated entirely in your browser. We never see your personal information.
  • GPA calculations are local. Your grades and course data never leave your browser. There is no account to hack and no database to breach.
  • Flashcards are stored locally. Your study sets are saved in your browser's localStorage, not on our servers. Clear your browser data anytime to remove them.
