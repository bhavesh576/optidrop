GST-Compliant Invoicing Made Simple

Since the introduction of GST in India, every registered business must issue GST-compliant invoices. This means including your GSTIN, the recipient's GSTIN (for B2B), HSN or SAC codes for goods and services, and a clear breakdown of CGST/SGST or IGST depending on whether the transaction is intra-state or inter-state.

The OptiDrop Invoice Generator handles all of this automatically. You enter your business details once, add line items with HSN codes and applicable tax rates, and the generator calculates the correct GST breakdown. The resulting PDF invoice includes every field required for GST compliance and filing.

  • GSTIN fields for supplier and recipient
  • HSN/SAC code support for every line item
  • CGST/SGST/IGST automatic calculation based on transaction type
  • Invoice numbering with customizable prefix and sequence
  • PDF download ready for GST filing and record-keeping
