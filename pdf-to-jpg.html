OptiDrop's PDF to JPG Extractor converts each page of your PDF document into a separate, high-quality JPG image. Whether you need to extract a single page, grab presentation slides as images, convert a scanned document into shareable pictures, or pull charts and graphs from a report, this tool handles it all — completely free and entirely in your browser. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server, making it safe for confidential documents like contracts, financial reports, and personal records.

Adjustable Quality and Resolution

Choose from three JPEG quality levels (High, Medium, Low) and three resolution scales (2x, 1.5x, 1x) to balance image clarity against file size. The default High quality at 2x scale produces crisp, print-ready images. For web use or email sharing where smaller files are preferred, Medium quality at 1.5x is a good compromise. All rendering happens client-side using PDF.js, ensuring fast processing and complete privacy.

Download Individual Pages or All as ZIP
