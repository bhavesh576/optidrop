Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Introduction

OptiDrop ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website at optidrop.online and all associated tools and services. We have designed OptiDrop with privacy as a fundamental principle — not an afterthought.

No Personal Data Collection

OptiDrop does not collect, store, or process any personal data. We do not require user accounts, email addresses, names, or any other identifying information to use our tools. You can use every tool on OptiDrop anonymously.

Browser-Side Processing

All tools on OptiDrop process data entirely within your browser using JavaScript. When you compress an image, convert a PDF, generate a QR code, or use any other tool, the processing happens locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers. This means your data never leaves your device during processing.

No Server Uploads

We want to be absolutely clear: no files, documents, images, or any content you process through OptiDrop tools are ever uploaded to our servers or any external servers. All processing is done client-side in your web browser. This is a core design principle of OptiDrop.

No User Accounts

OptiDrop does not have a user registration or login system. There are no user accounts, no passwords, and no user profiles. You can access and use all tools without providing any personal information.

localStorage

Some OptiDrop tools use your browser's localStorage feature to save form inputs for your convenience. For example, if you fill in your name in the Email Signature Generator, it will be saved locally so you do not have to re-enter it next time. OptiDrop also uses localStorage to remember your recently visited tools and your dark mode preference. New tools like the To-Do List, Goal Tracker, Budget Planner, Habit Tracker, Body Measurement Tracker, Plant Watering Reminder, Flashcard Generator, Quiz Generator, Coin Flip Simulator, Packing List, Itinerary Planner, Book Reading Tracker, Movie Watchlist, Feeding Schedule, Milestone Tracker, Medication Reminder, Blood Pressure Tracker, and Time Block Scheduler also store data locally in your browser. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device
  • Never leaves your browser
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage
  • Is prefixed with "optidrop_" for easy identification

Cookies and Analytics

OptiDrop uses Google Analytics (G-2TXPNV88YM) to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and device information
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • How you arrived at OptiDrop (referral source)

This data is aggregated and anonymous — it cannot be used to identify you personally. Google Analytics does not collect your name, email address, or any files you process. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

Advertising

OptiDrop displays advertisements through Google AdSense to support the platform and keep all tools free. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party Services

OptiDrop uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics (anonymous, aggregated data)
  • Google AdSense: Advertising (uses cookies for ad personalization)
  • Tailwind CSS CDN: Stylesheet delivery
  • Google Fonts: Font delivery (Inter typeface)

These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

OptiDrop does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal information from any user, this applies equally to all age groups.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of OptiDrop after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or OptiDrop's privacy practices, please contact us at contact@optidrop.online.
