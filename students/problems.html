What Is the Problem Bank?

The Problem Bank is a curated collection of real-world problems that students can solve by building tech projects. Unlike generic "project idea" lists, every problem here is something real people actually face — making your project meaningful, resume-worthy, and interview-ready.

How to Use It

Browse problems by category or difficulty. When you find one that interests you, read the context, reveal the hints, and start building. The hints guide your thinking without giving away the full solution — because the thinking is what makes you a better engineer.

For Hackathons

Look for problems marked "hackathon: 5" — these are scoped for 2-3 day builds with impressive demos. They're perfect for weekend hackathons where judges value real-world impact.

For Resumes

Problems with high "resume" scores are projects that recruiters want to hear about. They demonstrate problem-solving, technical skills, and impact — the three things companies look for.
